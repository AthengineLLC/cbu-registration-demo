# Screen: Team Management, athengine system taste test

- Who uses it: Addison, deciding whether to adopt the athengine design system
- What they are doing: comparing the new system against the navy look using a
  screen they already know, with identical real content
- What they see: the Team Management list (grad year groups, team tables, New
  team button) built entirely from CHEATSHEET.md components, styled live by the
  production stylesheet at cbu.athengine.com/proto.css
- What happens after: Addison keeps, blends, or rejects the new system for
  future mockups. This page is a reference, not a flow to build.
- Out of scope: interactivity, the remaining graduation years, the sidebar
  (see app-shell.html for that)
