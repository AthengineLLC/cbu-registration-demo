# Screen: Parent-facing team schedule

- Who uses it: a parent on a phone, checking where to be and when
- What they are doing: viewing their team's full season - practices,
  tournaments, and games - as a list or a month calendar
- What they see: a chronological list grouped by day (tournament banners, game
  rows with time, opponent, field, jersey, probable pitchers, result) or a
  month calendar with the same items as chips; tapping a calendar item opens a
  detail card. Every location has a map link built from the street address +
  city + state that coaches entered in Team Management.
- What happens after: nothing to submit - this page is read-only. Tournaments
  whose schedule is not released yet say so and tell parents when to check back.
- Out of scope: arrival times (later), RSVPs / availability, editing anything,
  multi-team households, notifications
- Note for Ben: this is the payoff page for the structured game data (complex /
  field / city / state / street address, jersey, ordered rotation) captured in
  the Team Management Events / Schedule tab. Everything shown here flows from
  what coaches enter there. Data in this mockup is hardcoded to mirror the
  Menendez sample team.
